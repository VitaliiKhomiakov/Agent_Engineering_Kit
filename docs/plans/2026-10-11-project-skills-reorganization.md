  final coverage must detect this, including section-level and code-span routes.

  PS-04 relocated/nested fixtures must fail without correct reference adaptation.

  PS-05 unrelated-task observations must catch unnecessary activation/reading.

  same-name paths, B/C/N conflicts, missing base and interrupted recovery.

  and PS-05 present/absent observations verify the effective procedure.
- A successful historical pilot is reused as proof for changed packaging: PS-05
  requires current matched observations; PS-07 reviews evidence identity and limits.
